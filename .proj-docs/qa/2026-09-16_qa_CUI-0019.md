### M2 / M3 詳情 — 新開 CUI-0028

W-026 改寫後嘅 `SAMPLE_KEY_SEPARATOR` docstring 明文寫：

> What makes the key injective is that one of its two halves cannot contain this character …
> Let that change and both orders break together, and the key stops matching silently rather than raising.

**呢句係啱嘅，但冇測試守住。** 我兩邊都驗過：

**(a) 個 invariant 係真嘅。** 另建一個 export-shaped DB，activity id 用**變長純十進制**
（`"1"`, `"01"`, `"10"`, `"2"`, `"20"`, `"002"`，每條 60 行），`points=7`：

| separator | 結果 |
|---|---|
| `":"` | ✅ 六條 track 全部啱 |
| `"0"` | ❌ activity `"01"` 回 **10 行**（應該 7）—— key 撞咗，攞多咗行 |
| `""` | ❌ 同上 |

碰撞：`position=5, id="01"` → `"5001"`；`position=50, id="1"` → `"5001"`。
**靜靜雞攞錯行，唔會報錯**，同 docstring 預言嘅一模一樣。
